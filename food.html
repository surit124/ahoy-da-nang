Local Food in Da Nang

Da Nang is a great place to experience Vietnamese food. Visitors can enjoy local noodle dishes, fresh seafood, and many other popular specialties throughout the city.

Mi Quang

Mi Quang is a popular noodle dish from Central Vietnam. It is served with noodles, herbs, peanuts, meat or seafood, and a small amount of flavorful broth.

Fresh Seafood

Da Nang's coastal location makes fresh seafood a popular choice for visitors. Local restaurants offer many types of fish, shrimp, crab, shellfish, and other seafood dishes.

Banh Xeo

Banh Xeo is a crispy Vietnamese pancake often filled with shrimp, pork, and bean sprouts. It is commonly served with fresh herbs and dipping sauce.
