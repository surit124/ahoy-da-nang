Featured Attractions

Dragon Bridge

See one of Da Nang's most famous landmarks light up over the Han River.

My Khe Beach

Relax along a beautiful stretch of coastline with soft sand and clear water.

Marble Mountains

Explore caves, temples, viewpoints, and historic sites just south of the city.

Why Visit Da Nang?

Da Nang combines beautiful beaches, delicious food, Vietnamese culture, and easy access to many memorable attractions.

  • Beautiful beaches
  • Delicious local food
  • Rich culture

Popular Local Foods

Mi Quang

A famous Da Nang noodle dish served with herbs, peanuts, meat or seafood, and a flavorful broth.

Seafood

Enjoy fresh seafood at local restaurants along Da Nang's beautiful coastline.

Banh Xeo

Try crispy Vietnamese pancakes filled with shrimp, pork, bean sprouts, and fresh herbs.

Travel Tips

Best Time

Plan your visit during pleasant weather for beaches and outdoor activities.

Transportation

Use taxis, ride-hailing apps, or motorbikes to explore the city.

Budget

Da Nang offers many affordable options for food, transportation, and sightseeing.

Etiquette

Be respectful at temples and local sites, and dress appropriately when visiting religious places.
