Top Attractions in Da Nang

Da Nang offers a variety of attractions for visitors to explore. From famous landmarks and beautiful beaches to natural and cultural sites, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

Experience Da Nang

Watch this short video for a closer look at the beauty and atmosphere visitors can experience in Da Nang.

Dragon Bridge

Dragon Bridge is one of Da Nang's most recognizable landmarks. The bridge crosses the Han River and is known for its unique dragon-shaped design.

My Khe Beach

My Khe Beach is a popular place to enjoy the coastline of Da Nang. Visitors can relax on the beach, enjoy the ocean, and spend time outdoors.

Marble Mountains

Marble Mountains is a well-known attraction with caves, temples, viewpoints, and natural scenery. Visitors can explore the area while learning more about its cultural importance.
